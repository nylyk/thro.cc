Talk like you're in the same room

Low-latency voice for up to 30 people. Push-to-talk or voice activation, with echo cancellation and noise suppression.

Screens worth staring at

Share up to 1440p60 with system audio, five at a time. Everyone watches only the shares they pick.

Lips sealed

Everything you say and share is end-to-end encrypted. The server passes it along but holds no key.

Hosting you can sleep through

One server binary and one data directory. Friends join with an address and an invite code.
